## What the NonCommercial term means for this project

Because the bundled artwork is NonCommercial, **any distribution of this
application that includes `assets/` is non-commercial**. Concretely, this project
must not be sold, bundled with paid software, monetized with advertising, placed
behind a paywall, or used to promote a commercial product or service.

The ShareAlike term applies to the assets and to adaptations of them. It does not
convert the MIT-licensed source code to CC BY-NC-SA; the two licenses coexist,
each governing its own files.
